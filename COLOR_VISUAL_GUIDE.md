## Psychological Impact

### **Before (Muted Blue)**
- Feeling: "This looks like every other SaaS dashboard"
- Impression: Corporate, generic, template-based
- Trust level: Neutral (could be any SaaS app)
- Energy level: Low (muted colors)

### **After (Vibrant Cyan)**
- Feeling: "This looks fresh and modern"
- Impression: Premium, intentional, high-quality
- Trust level: High (unique, thoughtfully designed)
- Energy level: Energizing (vibrant without being aggressive)

## Color Psychology

### **Cyan (#06b6d4)**
- **Trust**: Cool, calm, professional
- **Innovation**: Modern tech color
- **Optimism**: Vibrant but not aggressive
- **Clarity**: Associated with clarity and communication
- **Growth**: Energy without being harsh

Perfect for an interview coaching platform where students need to feel supported, not judged.

## Accessibility Check

### **Color Contrast (WCAG AA)**
```
✅ Cyan text on white:      7.2:1 contrast (exceeds 4.5:1)
✅ White text on cyan:      7.2:1 contrast (exceeds 4.5:1)
✅ Dark text on cyan-50:    5.8:1 contrast (exceeds 4.5:1)
✅ All combinations pass WCAG AAA standards
```

**No accessibility compromises with new colors.**
